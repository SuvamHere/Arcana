An interactive, educational landscape experience built in Godot 4. This project serves as a visual, clickable companion to our existing trivia and geoguesser platforms, allowing users to explore a beautiful panoramic view of Marina Bay and learn interesting facts at their own pace.

## Overview

The goal of this project is to create a relaxing environment where players can learn about Singapore's history, architecture, and the science behind a sunset. There are no timers, no fail states, and no pressure. Users simply click around the landscape to uncover educational popups. 

The screen is divided into invisible, clickable zones that correspond to different categories of trivia:

- **The Cityscape Zone**: Clicking on the buildings or iconic landmarks (like the Merlion, Marina Barrage, or Gardens by the Bay) reveals historical and architectural facts about Singapore's famous locations.
- **The Sky Zone**: Clicking on the sky brings up facts about sunset science (such as Rayleigh scattering and the troposphere) and photography techniques (like the rule of thirds and aperture).
- **The Water Zone**: Clicking on the water reveals insights into color theory, design balance, and reflections.

## Features

- **Clean, Clutter-Free UI**: The landscape is entirely clear of markers or buttons, encouraging natural exploration.
- **Dynamic Fact Database**: A robust, built-in dictionary containing over 60 distinct educational topics.
- **Smooth Animations**: Popups and UI elements animate cleanly in and out to provide a polished feel.
- **Trivia Integration**: The facts presented in the game perfectly mirror the questions and answers available on our main trivia platform.

## How to Play

1. Launch the game.
2. Read the brief introduction to understand the context.
3. Click anywhere on the sky, city, or water to bring up a unique fact.
4. Read the information panel, close it, and click somewhere else to learn something new!
