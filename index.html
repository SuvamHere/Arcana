Golden Hour
A tribute to the bay at dusk

Where the skyline meets the sunset.

Every evening, Marina Bay turns the same trick: glass towers catch the last light, the water doubles it, and for a few minutes the whole city glows amber. This is a small corner of the internet built for that hour.

Landmarks worth the golden hour

Four fixtures of the bay that shape its skyline — and four of the ideas the trivia game will test you on.

Skypark

Marina Bay Sands

Three towers joined by a boat-shaped platform, with the city's most photographed infinity pool riding on top.

Observation wheel

Singapore Flyer

One of the tallest observation wheels ever built, turning slow enough to make the sunset last a little longer.

Gardens by the bay

The Supertrees

Vertical gardens built to look like trees from another century, lit up just as the real sky starts to dim.

Waterfront museum

ArtScience Museum

A lotus-shaped silhouette at the water's edge — the calmest shape on a skyline full of glass and steel.
